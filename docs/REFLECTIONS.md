# Reflecting on a real interview

A private debrief for an interview the learner recorded on an application: what they were asked,
what felt clear or hard, what they noticed, what they said they'd do, and what they want to
practise. It turns their own words into next steps they can choose. It's separate from simulated
interviews and from any feedback CodeCoach generates, and it never claims to know what an
interviewer thought.

## Writing it

"Reflect on this interview" on any event of an application. Every part is optional and unknown
is fine:

- Questions you were asked, topics that came up, and what you want to practise (one per line).
- What felt clear, and what was difficult.
- **Observations**, each marked as **what happened** ("they asked for another example") or
  **what I think it meant** ("they didn't like me"). The page explains the difference, and
  nothing is ever suggested from an interpretation.
- What you said you'd do (follow-up commitments). CodeCoach never sends anything.
- Stories you used, chosen from your story bank (stable ids).

Guidance at the top: paraphrase rather than copy questions; leave out test content you agreed not
to share, employer secrets, and anything identifying patients, clients, colleagues or
interviewers; audio isn't part of this, and interviews shouldn't be recorded without consent. If
the text looks like it contains an email address, a phone number or a long ID number, a gentle
warning says so; nothing is blocked.

Saving is revision-checked (a stale tab gets `409` and can choose to save over), unsaved words are
kept on the device until saved, a failed save keeps everything on the page, and reopening offers
"Restore them". Leaving with unsaved changes asks first. The role, employer, stage, event title and
date are copied on the first save, so an old reflection still reads correctly after the
application is edited or archived.

## Next steps, chosen by the learner

"Suggest next steps" builds suggestions only from what was written, deterministically:

- **Practice**: for each topic, practice wish, question or difficulty, the best-matching question
  in the learner's career (by words in its title and tags), never the same question twice.
- **Gap**: a topic or practice wish with no matching content says so ("No practice here yet for
  …") instead of inventing a lesson. It can be kept as a note.
- **Note**: follow-up commitments, to keep in the notebook.

Nothing is applied until the learner chooses. Each suggestion can be edited, dismissed (and
brought back), or added to **the notebook** (a note tagged "interview reflection"), **the
schedule** (a practice session on a chosen date) or **Saved** (a bookmark). Accepting twice
returns the same result and never creates a second note, entry or bookmark. Suggesting again adds
only new suggestions and never resurrects a dismissed one or changes an accepted one. The adaptive
plan has no hand-added steps, so "add to plan" isn't offered; the schedule covers "do this on a
day". No AI provider is used. Feeling unsure about something never marks a skill as failed.

## Outcomes

"What happened next" records what the learner has been told: not known yet, waiting to hear,
another round, offer, not selected, or I withdrew. Each change is kept with its time. The
application moves to the matching stage only when the learner ticks "Also move the application
to …" (another round → interviewing, offer → offer, not selected → closed, withdrawn →
withdrawn); waiting never moves it, and nothing infers a rejection from a slow reply. Outcomes
aren't used in practice metrics, analytics or readiness, and there's no hiring-probability
score.

## Finding reflections

"Your interview reflections" (linked from Applications) lists them newest first, with private
search across the text, an outcome filter and an "include archived applications" switch; the
filters are in the URL, so Back returns to the same list. Search runs over the learner's own rows
on the server; nothing is copied into the notebook or indexed elsewhere.

## Privacy

Reflections and their actions are owner-only (`404` for anyone else), sent with `Cache-Control:
no-store`, never logged, included in the data export (`interview_reflections`, with each action and
what it created), and deleted with the event, the application or the account. Notes and
schedule entries created from accepted steps belong to those features and follow their rules.
